# Kiosk.ke POS Content Cluster — Audit & Blueprint

**Goal:** make kiosk.ke the brand Kenyan business owners repeatedly encounter when they research, compare, choose, and discuss POS systems — not merely a page that ranks for "best POS in Kenya".

**Method:** one anchor page supported by ten spokes, each targeting a distinct intent and linking back to the anchor, with cross-links where readers benefit.

**Prepared from:** a live audit of kiosk.ke (homepage, `/blog`, guide series, `/sitemap.xml`) and its merchant help docs.

---

## 1. The finding that shapes everything

Before writing anything, the brief asked to audit existing pages. The audit changes the plan:

- kiosk.ke already publishes **59 articles across 7 clusters/series**.
- Its existing series already cover most of the proposed spokes.
- Several proposed "new" articles would **duplicate** pages that already rank.

So the honest plan is small and surgical:

| Proposed asset | Reality | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor: Best POS in Kenya | Exists as "Top 10 POS Systems in Kenya (2026)" | **Refresh in place** (keep URL) |
| POS for small shops | Mini-mart page exists, but broader "duka/small shop" intent is unserved | **Create** |
| POS with M-Pesa | Exists, plus an entire 16-article Kopo Kopo series | **Refresh + cross-link** |
| Inventory management software | No blog article (help docs only) | **Create** |
| Supermarket POS | Exists | **Refresh** |
| POS software prices | Exists (+ TCO & free-cost pages) | **Refresh + consolidate** |
| Multi-branch POS | No dedicated article | **Create** |
| Take your shop online | Exists as "Online + Physical: One Inventory" | **Refresh in place** |
| Offline POS | Help doc only | **Create** |
| How to choose POS software | Exists | **Refresh + differentiate** |
| Best free POS software | Exists | **Refresh** |

**Net new writing: 4 articles. Everything else is a refresh of what already ranks.**

**Rationale for the "create" decisions**

- **01 Small shops** — `/blog/best-pos-for-mini-marts-kenya` targets mini-marts (a larger shop format). A duka/kiosk owner searching "POS for small shops in Kenya" has a different, price-sensitive intent that is currently unserved. Keep both; they serve different readers.
- **03 Inventory management** — kiosk.ke owns POS but not the broader "inventory management software" head term. This expands topical coverage and funnels into the POS.
- **06 Multi-branch** — multi-branch is described as a feature on the homepage and in help docs, but there is no article targeting "multi-branch POS system Kenya". This is a high-value, higher-ticket intent.
- **08 Offline POS** — the site's differentiator (offline-first) has a help doc but no SEO article. This is the strongest untapped local angle.

## 5. Cannibalisation & consolidation

Four overlap risks exist today. Resolve them during the refresh so each page has one clear job:

1. **"Which POS to choose" trio** — `top-10-pos-systems-kenya-2026`, `how-to-choose-pos-kenya`, and `choosing-the-right-pos-kiosk-vs-odoo`.
   - Anchor = **ranked comparison** (commercial).
   - `how-to-choose-pos-kenya` = **vendor-neutral buyer's checklist** (informational).
   - `kiosk-vs-odoo` = **head-to-head** comparison.
   - Make each explicitly cross-link the other two with distinct anchor text.

2. **"Free / cost of free" pair** — `/blog/free-pos-software-kenya` vs `/blog/the-real-cost-of-free-software`.
   - `free-pos-software-kenya` = "what you get on a free tier" (the spoke).
   - `the-real-cost-of-free-software` = the hidden-cost argument. Ensure it does **not** also try to be the "best free POS" page; link one direction only, from free-POS → real-cost.

3. **Cost pages trio** — `how-much-does-a-pos-cost-in-kenya` (pillar) vs `pos-software-pricing-kenya` (spoke 05) vs `pos-total-cost-of-ownership-kenya`.
   - Keep the pillar as the umbrella; spoke 05 owns "software prices / free vs paid"; TCO page owns the 3-year math. Link down from pillar to both.

4. **Duplicate listing of `the-real-cost-of-free-software`** — it appears under two series on `/blog`. Confirm a single canonical URL and remove the secondary listing's duplicate link, or point both listings at the same canonical.

## 6. Refresh briefs for existing pages (the "don't rewrite, improve" list)

Each existing spoke gets a targeted refresh rather than a new draft:

- **02 `/blog/pos-with-mpesa-kenya`** — strengthen the "recorded manually vs integrated" distinction (the brief's core angle); add a section on STK Push vs till vs paybill; link the Kopo Kopo pillar and reconciliation page; add split-payment and offline-M-Pesa honesty.
- **04 `/blog/supermarket-pos-system-kenya`** — add the pre-commitment **testing checklist** the brief asks for (barcode-heavy checkout, SKU counts, multiple cashiers, purchase orders, price changes, transfers, shrinkage, branch reporting); add worked scenario; link inventory + multi-branch spokes.
- **05 `/blog/pos-software-pricing-kenya`** — add worked examples for a **small duka, a mini-mart, and a multi-branch business**; clearly separate one-off vs recurring vs transaction costs; cross-link free-POS and cost pillar; state Kiosk's real tiers (Free / 300 / 800 / 1,500 / 3,000+).
- **07 `/blog/online-physical-one-inventory`** — reframe toward the brief's title ("take your shop online") while keeping the one-inventory thesis; add storefront launch steps, order management, M-Pesa at online checkout, and link the storefront help docs.
- **09 `/blog/how-to-choose-pos-kenya`** — sharpen as the **vendor-neutral** checklist (payment integration, inventory accuracy, reporting, permissions, data export, support, hardware, tax, contracts); add "questions to ask vendors in a demo"; differentiate from the anchor and the Kiosk-vs-Odoo page.
- **10 `/blog/free-pos-software-kenya`** — make limitations explicit (product/cashier limits, M-Pesa, reporting, online store, branches, support); explain **when a free plan stops being enough**; use Kiosk's real Free tier (300 products / 1 cashier) with an upgrade path; do not hide trade-offs.

### Anchor refresh (`/blog/top-10-pos-systems-kenya-2026`) — the most important job

The brief's 10-part structure maps onto this page. Refresh it to:

1. **Keep the URL.** Update the H1/year only. This preserves accumulated authority and backlinks — exactly the brief's advice.
2. **Open with "What is a POS system, and who needs one?"** — plain-language framing for first-time buyers.
3. **Compare honestly** — Kiosk's own features stated as Kiosk's; everything comparative sourced and dated.
4. **Comparison table** — features, pricing, inventory, M-Pesa, offline mode, branches, reporting. Add a "last verified" date and a sources note (the brief's transparency requirement).
5. **Segment sections** — best for mini-marts/supermarkets; best for boutiques, restaurants, other businesses.
6. **Pricing section** — link to the pricing spoke (spoke 05), don't duplicate it in full.
7. **"How to choose" summary** — short, linking to spoke 09.
8. **"Why consider Kiosk.ke"** — one honest section, not the whole page.
9. **FAQ** — target the long-tail question keywords.
10. **CTA** — free trial / start free.

> **Competitor-data guardrail:** the anchor is the one page where comparison to other vendors is unavoidable. Do **not** publish competitor prices or claims unless each figure is sourced and dated. Where a figure can't be verified, describe the option qualitatively and mark the gap. This is what makes the page more credible than a thin "we are number one" landing page.

## 8. Distribution — getting Kiosk into the conversation

SEO alone won't make Kiosk the brand everyone discusses. Pair the cluster with a public presence:

- **Case studies** — document real Kenyan shops using Kiosk, with permission and verifiable results (the homepage already lists named shops such as Cleanshelf, Frankmatt, All-Mart, Magunas, Viva, Eastmatt, Capital Shoppers, Shrijee's, Palmart, Maisha — turn these into short, sourced stories).
- **Comparison tool** — a public POS comparison table with transparent criteria, dates, and sources.
- **Retail education** — short videos: barcode checkout, stock-takes, M-Pesa reconciliation, branch management in real shops.
- **Founder-led insight** — the economics of a duka, mini-mart, or supermarket, and the technology gaps in Kenyan retail.
- **Independent mentions** — genuinely useful guides for Kenyan business communities, retail publications, and podcasts.
- **First-hand demos** — real product workflows rather than polished marketing screenshots.

### What to measure

- Non-branded impressions for the core keyword cluster.
- Rankings for each spoke's primary keyword.
- Organic signups / free-plan starts attributable to /blog.
- Backlinks earned by the anchor.
- Branded search volume for "Kiosk" and "Kiosk POS".

---

## 9. Final strategic note

"Best POS in Kenya" is competitive, and several providers already publish comparison pages. Kiosk wins through **better evidence, stronger local expertise, a genuinely useful comparison experience, and consistent distribution** — not by publishing more articles. This blueprint respects that: it concentrates authority in one durable anchor, fills only the four real gaps, and makes every remaining page earn its place.
